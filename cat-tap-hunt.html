🐱 The Ultimate Hunting Training Game for Your Cat!

Turn your phone into the ultimate cat toy! Cat Tap Hunt is a thrilling hunting simulation designed to awaken your cat's natural instincts. Watch your feline friend tap, swipe, and pounce on realistic prey.

🎯 How to Play

• Choose your prey from unique animals
• Your cat catches the target
• Score points and watch the hunt unfold
• Customize speed, size, and number of prey

🐾 Exciting Prey Animals

Bird, Squirrel, Frog, Porcupine, Mouse, Fox, Praying Mantis, Rabbit, Mole, Salamander, Cockroach.

✨ Key Features

🎮 Infinite Gameplay
🎥 Automatic Video Recording
📊 Score Tracking
⚙️ Fully Customizable
📳 Vibration Feedback
🎵 Immersive Audio
🌙 Beautiful Visuals

📹 Video Recording Feature

Auto-delete old recordings (1-30 days), select/delete multiple clips, share to WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook, thumbnail previews.

🎨 Customize

Character Size (150px–320px), Movement Speed (slow to lightning fast), Character Count (1–8 prey), Per-animal settings.

🐈 Perfect For

Cat owners, kitten training, keeping indoor cats active, bonding time.

💡 Pro Tips

Start with slower prey like Mouse for kittens, increase speed as your cat improves, use a screen protector, supervise play sessions, try different prey.

🔒 Privacy & Permissions

Camera/Microphone only for optional video recording. Storage for saving clips. No personal data collection. No ads during gameplay.
